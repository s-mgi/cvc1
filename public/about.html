About CVC Group

Who we are. A construction company built around craft.

Two decades of building environments for the brands that shape how people live, eat, shop, and gather.

Our Story

Builders at heart, partners by trade.

CVC Group was founded on a simple belief: a space is only as good as the people who build it. Since 2003, we've delivered construction projects across hospitality, retail, and commercial sectors — from boutique restaurants to flagship retail rollouts to corporate workplaces.

What sets us apart isn't size — it's repeat clients, on-time turnover, and a network of trades who pick up the phone when we call.

What We Stand For

Three principles guide every project.

Craft

Construction is detail work. We sweat the millwork, the finishes, the punch list — because guests and customers feel the difference.

Partnership

We work as part of your team — architects, designers, ownership, operations. Clear communication and shared timelines, every step.

Precision

Budgets and schedules are commitments, not estimates. We plan early, build accurately, and hand over what we said we would.
